Independent since 1989.

First published in 1989, United We Stand is the authentic voice of Manchester United supporters. Edited by Andy Mitten, a lifelong Red who watches United home and away, UWS delivers honest, informed opinion from match-going fans.

Award-winning and best-selling, with over 300 issues published and named British Fanzine of the Year in 2012 and 2019, UWS is proudly and completely independent — not beholden to advertisers. Each of the 11 issues per season features 44 pages of analysis, opinion, and the truth about Manchester United.

Influential. Incisive. Irreverent.
